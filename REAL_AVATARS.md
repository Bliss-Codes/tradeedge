# Real portrait avatars

Replaced symbol/icon presets with 8 portrait-style generated SVG avatars:
emerald, cobalt, violet, amber, rose, teal, gold and navy.

The portraits are bundled under `public/avatars/` and selected through the
existing profile avatar field. Uploading a personal photo still works as before.
No Supabase, persistence, account, trade, or analytics logic was changed.
