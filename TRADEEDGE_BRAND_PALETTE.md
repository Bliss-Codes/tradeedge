# TradeEdge Brand Palette

Primary palette based on the supplied reference:

- #FFFDEE — warm white / light canvas
- #E2FBCE — soft lime green
- #E3EF26 — signature lime accent
- #076653 — primary brand green
- #0C342C — deep green surface
- #06231D — deepest green background

Dark theme:
#06231D → #0C342C → #076653 with #E3EF26 as the high-attention accent.

Light theme:
#FFFDEE / #FFFFFF surfaces with #076653 as the primary brand color and #E3EF26 used selectively.

This is presentation-only. No Supabase, persistence, trading logic, or database files were changed.
